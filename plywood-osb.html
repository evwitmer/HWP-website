FAQ

Common questions

What's the difference between plywood and OSB?

Plywood is made from thin wood veneers glued in alternating layers. OSB (oriented strand board) is made from compressed wood strands. OSB usually costs less; plywood holds fasteners a little better and handles moisture better at the edges.

What thicknesses do you carry?

We sell full 4×8 sheets in common thicknesses. Stock changes, so call or send a quote request with the thickness and quantity you need.

Can I buy sheet goods for my own crating or building project?

Yes. Contractors, farms, manufacturers and homeowners all buy plywood and OSB from us.
