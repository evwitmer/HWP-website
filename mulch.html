FAQ

Common questions

What kind of mulch do you sell?

We sell natural ground wood mulch produced at our Kendallville yard from clean recovered wood.

How is bulk mulch sold?

Mulch is sold in bulk by the load. Pick it up at our yard or have it delivered by dump trailer. Call for current pricing and availability.

Do you deliver mulch?

Yes. We deliver within roughly 100 miles of Kendallville, including Fort Wayne, Auburn, Angola and Goshen.

Do you sell to landscapers and contractors?

Yes. Landscapers, contractors, municipalities and homeowners all buy mulch from us. Ask about recurring loads for the season.
