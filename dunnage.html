FAQ

Common questions

What is dunnage?

Dunnage is the lumber used to block, brace and support cargo so it doesn't shift during shipping. It's also used to lift loads off the floor so forks and straps can get underneath.

What sizes of dunnage do you sell?

Common requests are 2×4, 4×4 and 4×6 cut to length, but we can cut to the dimensions your loads require. Call with your sizes and quantity.

Can you cut dunnage to a specific length?

Yes. Cut-to-length dunnage is one of the most common requests we get from trucking, steel and manufacturing customers.
