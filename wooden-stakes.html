FAQ

Common questions

Does Horizon Wood Products sell wooden stakes?

Yes, through our sister company The Stake Yard in Kendallville. The Stake Yard handles stake orders, pricing and jobsite delivery.

What kinds of stakes are available?

Concrete breakaway stakes, survey hubs and lath, garden and landscape stakes, and foundation-grade stakes for retaining walls and structural concrete. Custom sizes and large quantities can be milled locally.

How do I order stakes?

Request pricing at thestakeyard.com, call The Stake Yard at (260) 450-8511, or email orders@thestakeyard.com.
